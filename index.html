Year by year

2026
Heavy disease year. Every spray dosed on the wrong area (728 m²), so roughly half rate, and on a monthly cadence. Downy mildew across ~80% of the vineyard in July, black rot mummies on almost every vine by late August, powdery mildew on the bunch stems and Botrytis at harvest. About 90% of clusters were affected to some degree.
Harvest 19 Sep · ~150 L of must at 20.2 °Brix, pH 3.36 · ~120 L of wine after racking · dry on 5 Oct at ≈12% ABV.
2027
Plan: full rates on 1,400 m², 8 spray sessions, a 10–14-day cadence through bloom. Target ~1 t of fruit. Sanitation over winter, Tebustar added early against black rot, two Mospilan sprays against the grape berry moth.

Problems and lessons

What went wrong in 2026 — ranked

  1. Half-rate sprays. The 728 m² figure halved every dose and every water volume.
  2. Monthly cadence across the critical window. Berries are susceptible to powdery and black rot from pre-bloom to ~4–5 weeks after set; a 30-day gap leaves 2–3 weeks unprotected each cycle.
  3. Powdery on the bunch stems → split berries → Botrytis. The last powdery-active spray (15 Aug, half rate) ran out before a warm, dry September.
  4. Black rot read as downy. Not confirmed until the mummies showed in late August.
  5. Bragadiru sprayed on Buzău's calendar, though it runs 1–2 weeks ahead.
  6. Misleading signals. Canopy and Brix looked fine while the crop was lost inside the bunches.
Possible contributor, unconfirmed: an agronomist suggested insects play a part. Grape berry moth larvae bore into berries and open them to Botrytis and sour rot. No insect damage was recorded in 2026 — check clusters for webbing and bored berries in 2027.
What went right: healthy vines with vigorous clean regrowth; sorting kept the must clean; pH 3.36 without acid; the ferment finished dry and clean.

Downy partner for sessions 2 and 4: Delan Pro (decided 22 Nov 2026), 38.1 mL per tank. Labelled for downy mildew and black rot on wine grapes; PHI 42 days; max 4 sprays a year.

The three sprays that decide the crop are ★ 2–4: pre-bloom → end of bloom → shot-size berries, 10–14 days apart, late May to early July. That window is where 2026 was lost.

Contingencies
  • Wet June / black rot before bloom: session 4 → Universalis + phosphonate partner; session 5 → Microthiol 38.1 g + Folpan 20.3 g + Mospilan 3.1 g.
  • Universalis allowed only once: session 5 → Microthiol + Folpan + Mospilan.
  • Wet August: add Orondis Ultra 8.5 mL to session 6 (second and last use).
  • Heatwave on a sulfur day: drop the sulfur, spray at dawn.
  • Moth flight off-schedule: move the Mospilan to the flight date; a third moth spray is Laser 240 SC, never a third Mospilan.
  • Product no longer sold or authorised: rebuild the rotation; don't improvise from the shelf.
Rotation rules
  • A single-site FRAC group at most twice a season, never in consecutive sprays, never above the label maximum.
  • FRAC 11 at sessions 2 and 5 · FRAC 3 at sessions 1 and 3 · IRAC 4A at sessions 5 and 6.
  • Never fosetyl (Aliette, Mikal Flash, Profiler) with copper. Folpet once per tank. No sulfur above ~28–30 °C, none within 6 weeks of picking. No copper after mid-July.
  • No insecticide around bloom; mow flowering cover first.
